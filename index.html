Booth Design

Booth design is the art and plan of building a temporary physical space for a brand at a trade show, exhibition, or public event.


Its goal is to attract visitors, showcase products and communicate your brand effectively in a busy exhibition environment.

YOUR SELECTION

Shell Scheme

A shell scheme is a ready-made modular exhibition space provided by event organisers.


It generally includes aluminium framing, wall panels, fascia branding, basic flooring, lighting and power provision.
